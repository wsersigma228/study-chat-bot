# Domain rules

Schedules require recognized text, a supported date basis and a confirmed publisher. A missing schedule does not mean that classes are cancelled. Partial or ambiguous text remains reviewable.

Estimated schedules are presentation only. After the evening cutoff, the implementation searches the same weekday two weeks earlier, then one week earlier; Monday's cutoff is Friday evening. Estimates retain their source date and never become verified database rows.

Homework can use explicit subject text and approved source associations. A source may relate to several subjects. Conflicting or incomplete evidence remains uncertain. Deadlines require explicit date evidence; relative deadlines remain text rather than invented dates. Missing deadlines stay unspecified.

Unavailable attachments are a normal state. No text is invented from a photo or document. Owner corrections take precedence over subsequent automatic parsing.

The collector reads the source group. Bot command groups and a publication channel must be configured separately. Access controls and owner-only review remain in force even for synthetic demonstrations.
